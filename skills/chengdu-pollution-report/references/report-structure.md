# Chengdu Pollution Report Structure

Use this outline for reports similar to Chengdu pollution process assessment documents. Keep sections that are supported by data; omit or explicitly mark unsupported advanced sections.

## Title

Use a concrete title:

```text
Chengdu {pollutant} Pollution-Process Assessment for {time range}
Cause Analysis and Emergency Emission-Reduction Recommendations for the Chengdu {pollutant} Episode, {time range}
Chengdu Air-Quality Characteristics and Emission-Reduction Analysis for {time range}
```

Translate the title naturally when the report language is not English.

## 1. Overall Air-Quality Conditions

Write:

- requested period and pollutant;
- citywide concentration/AQI trend, peak time, improvement time, duration;
- pollution level or warning conclusion from the warning skill;
- heavier regions from CMAQ correction/hotspots/stations;
- one compact conclusion sentence.

Optional visuals:

- city/district pollution calendar;
- city mean concentration time series;
- corrected CMAQ spatial distribution;
- hotspot table by district/time.

## 2. Weather Situation and Meteorological Factors

Write from Windy.com and any available local meteorology:

- circulation/background: ridge/trough, basin static/stable situation, subsidence, transport direction, or other visible synoptic cues;
- surface factors: wind speed/direction, humidity, precipitation, temperature, cloud/solar radiation, visibility if available;
- dispersion/removal: stagnant wind, inversion/boundary layer, precipitation scavenging, or mixing improvement;
- pollutant-specific mechanism:
  - PM2.5: weak wind, high humidity, shallow boundary layer, secondary conversion, regional accumulation.
  - O3: high temperature, strong radiation, low cloud, afternoon photochemistry, downwind transport.

Avoid overstating upper-air diagnosis if Windy layers were not checked. When only surface layers are available, state that the inference is based on the visible near-surface wind, cloud, and precipitation fields.

## 3. Pollution Characteristics

Use evidence-backed subsections. Common patterns:

- Adverse meteorology combined with local accumulation or regional transport is an important external driver.
- The PM2.5 episode shows nighttime accumulation, rising humidity, and secondary-formation risk.
- The O3 episode shows afternoon peaks, downwind expansion, and photochemical production.
- Pollution increases are most evident in the identified high-risk areas or periods.

Only add component/source sections when data exist:

- particulate components: nitrate, sulfate, ammonium, organic matter, elemental carbon;
- VOCs/NOx: precursor ratio, VOCs group, NOx titration clues;
- source apportionment: mobile, industry, combustion, dust, solvent, biomass/open burning.

If no component/source data exist, write one limitation sentence instead of a fabricated analysis:

```text
Particle composition, VOC composition, and source-apportionment results were unavailable for this report. The pollution-characteristics analysis therefore relies on air-quality observations, corrected CMAQ results, and meteorological conditions.
```

## 4. Warning Assessment and Emergency Emission-Reduction Recommendations

Write:

- warning or response category and trigger logic;
- official plan-based measures from the warning skill;
- regional strengthened measures based on hotspots and emission characteristics;
- pollutant-specific priorities.

PM2.5 priority measures:

- industrial combustion and "one plant one policy";
- diesel trucks, freight channels, non-road machinery;
- construction dust, road dust, material yards;
- open burning, fireworks, catering oil fumes when relevant;
- night and early-morning accumulation patrols.

O3 priority measures:

- VOCs + NOx coordinated control;
- coating, printing, furniture, auto repair spraying, petrochemical/oil storage and transport;
- daytime high-temperature/high-radiation operation adjustment;
- traffic and non-road NOx reduction without excessive local NO titration assumptions;
- downwind districts and industrial parks.

## 5. Conclusions and Items Requiring Consultation

Close with:

- 2-4 bullet conclusions;
- recommended next action and warning/response caution;
- missing evidence or manual confirmation needs.

Use this section title for forecast or advice reports. For retrospective reports with validated emission-reduction simulations, use "Effectiveness Assessment of Emergency Emission-Reduction Measures"; otherwise do not claim quantified reduction effects.
