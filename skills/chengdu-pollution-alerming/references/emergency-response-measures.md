# Chengdu Heavy-Pollution Emergency Response Measures

Source: Chapter 3, “Emergency Response Measures,” of the Chengdu Heavy Pollution Weather Emergency Plan (2024 revision). This file is an English working translation structured for programmatic retrieval. The official Chinese plan prevails if wording differs.

The Chinese text at the start of each level-two heading is a stable parser key used by `calculate_aqi_warning.py`; do not remove or reorder it.

## III级响应/黄色预警 — Level III Response / Yellow Warning

### Health Protection

- Children, older adults, people with respiratory or cardiovascular/cerebrovascular diseases, and other chronic-disease patients should remain indoors where possible and use appropriate protection when going outside.
- The general public should reduce outdoor exercise and work time. Large outdoor public events should be suspended where appropriate, and outdoor personnel should use masks or shorten outdoor shifts.
- Education authorities should coordinate cancellation of outdoor activities at primary and secondary schools and kindergartens.
- Health authorities should coordinate additional relevant outpatient and emergency services and staffing.

### Voluntary Emission Reduction

- Encourage public transport and new-energy vehicles, reduce use of gasoline and gas vehicles, and switch off engines while parked.
- Encourage religious venues to reduce open burning of incense, candles, and paper offerings that generate smoke.
- Reduce the use of VOC-containing coatings, paints, solvents, raw materials, and products.
- Conduct weather-modification operations when conditions allow.

### Mandatory Emission Reduction

- Increase road and vegetation washing and dust removal in Key Management Areas and urbanized zones of General Management Areas. Conduct at least two washes between 22:00 and 06:00; stop water spraying below 4°C.
- All stone-processing enterprises must stop outdoor operations throughout the day, clean and wash sites at least three times daily, and spray uncovered yards at least six times daily.
- Prohibit particulate- and VOC-emitting operations and open storage or processing of dusty construction materials throughout the day in the specified management zones. Green benchmark sites and emergency-rescue projects follow the plan's exemptions.
- Construction sites citywide may not use China II or older non-road mobile machinery, except new-energy and emergency-rescue equipment.
- Auto-repair businesses in the specified management zones must stop paint mixing, spraying, baking, and sanding throughout the day, except certified green body-and-paint facilities.
- Listed air-polluting industrial enterprises must implement their Yellow Warning “one plant, one policy” measures, stop using China II or older non-road machinery, and shut equipment or lines scheduled for elimination that year.
- Restrict vehicles carrying spoil, sand, gravel, bagged cement, ready-mixed concrete, mortar, or asphalt in the specified zones. New-energy vehicles, compliant China VI vehicles, and plan-defined essential-service operations are exempt as specified.
- On workdays from 06:00 to 22:00, apply restrictions inside the Fourth Ring Road to older China III gasoline vehicles, China III or older diesel vehicles, China IV diesel trucks, and other vehicles under plate-number rules; exempt special-purpose vehicles.
- Landscaping operations in the specified zones may not use fuel-powered machinery except for emergencies.
- Prohibit fireworks, outdoor barbecues, and open burning of straw, leaves, weeds, or waste citywide; strengthen oversight of catering-oil-fume controls.
- Increase bus, metro, and other public-transport service frequency.

## II级响应/橙色预警 — Level II Response / Orange Warning

### Health Protection

- Apply the Yellow Warning health measures: protect susceptible groups indoors, reduce outdoor activity for the general population, cancel school outdoor activities, and strengthen outpatient and emergency medical coverage.

### Voluntary Emission Reduction

- Apply the Yellow Warning voluntary measures.
- Encourage public institutions and enterprises to use flexible arrangements such as leave rotation, staggered hours, and remote work according to pollution conditions.

### Mandatory Emission Reduction

- Increase washing and dust removal for roads, expressways, roadside trees, and greenbelts; conduct at least two washes between 22:00 and 06:00 and stop water spraying below 4°C.
- Stone-processing enterprises citywide must stop outdoor operations and meet cleaning, washing, and spraying requirements.
- Prohibit particulate- and VOC-emitting operations and open storage or processing of dusty construction materials citywide throughout the day. Construction sites may not use China III or older non-road machinery; new-energy and emergency-rescue machinery is exempt.
- Auto-repair businesses citywide must stop paint mixing, spraying, baking, and sanding throughout the day, except certified green body-and-paint facilities.
- Air-polluting industrial enterprises must implement Orange Warning “one plant, one policy” measures, stop using China III or older non-road machinery, and shut equipment or lines scheduled for elimination that year.
- Restrict vehicles carrying spoil, sand, gravel, bagged cement, ready-mixed concrete, mortar, or asphalt citywide. New-energy vehicles, compliant China VI vehicles, and plan-defined essential-service operations are exempt as specified.
- On workdays from 06:00 to 22:00, restrict China III gasoline vehicles, China IV or older diesel vehicles, and other vehicles subject to plate-number rules inside the Fourth Ring Road; exempt special-purpose vehicles.
- Yellow-license-plate trucks with a rated payload above 5 tonnes may not enter the Fourth Ring Road area at any time.
- Landscaping operations citywide may not use fuel-powered machinery except for emergencies.
- Prohibit fireworks, outdoor barbecues, and open burning of straw, leaves, weeds, or waste citywide; strengthen oversight of catering-oil-fume controls.
- Increase bus and metro frequency. Designated buses inside the Fourth Ring Road are free and metro fares receive a 20% discount, subject to the current policies of Tianfu Tong, Chengdu Metro, and Chengdu Bus services.

## I级响应/红色预警 — Level I Response / Red Warning

### Health Protection

- Susceptible groups should remain indoors where possible and use appropriate protection.
- The general public should reduce outdoor exercise and work time. Large outdoor public events should stop, and outdoor personnel should use protection and shorten outdoor shifts.
- Education authorities should guide eligible primary and secondary schools and kindergartens to suspend in-person classes according to air quality while maintaining remote learning.
- Health authorities should strengthen relevant outpatient, emergency, and treatment capacity.

### Voluntary Emission Reduction

- Apply the Orange Warning voluntary measures: green travel, reduced open burning, reduced VOC-containing material use, flexible working arrangements, and weather modification when feasible.

### Mandatory Emission Reduction

- Increase washing and dust removal for roads, expressways, roadside trees, and greenbelts; conduct at least two washes between 22:00 and 06:00 and stop water spraying below 4°C.
- Stone-processing enterprises citywide must stop outdoor operations and meet cleaning, washing, and spraying requirements.
- Prohibit particulate- and VOC-emitting operations and open storage or processing of dusty construction materials citywide throughout the day. Construction sites may not use China III or older non-road machinery; new-energy and emergency-rescue machinery is exempt.
- Auto-repair businesses citywide must stop paint mixing, spraying, baking, and sanding throughout the day.
- Air-polluting industrial enterprises must implement Red Warning “one plant, one policy” measures, stop using China III or older non-road machinery, and shut equipment or lines scheduled for elimination that year.
- Restrict vehicles carrying spoil, sand, gravel, bagged cement, ready-mixed concrete, mortar, or asphalt citywide. New-energy vehicles, emergency-rescue operations, continuous mass-concrete pours for rail-transit structures, and other plan-defined essential-service cases are exempt as specified.
- On workdays from 03:00 to 24:00, restrict China III gasoline vehicles and China IV or older diesel vehicles inside the Fourth Ring Road. Other vehicles alternate by odd/even plate number; exempt special-purpose vehicles.
- Yellow-license-plate trucks with a rated payload above 5 tonnes may not enter the Fourth Ring Road area at any time.
- Landscaping operations citywide may not use fuel-powered machinery except for emergencies.
- Prohibit fireworks, outdoor barbecues, and open burning of straw, leaves, weeds, or waste citywide; strengthen oversight of catering-oil-fume controls.
- Increase bus and metro frequency. Designated buses inside the Fourth Ring Road are free and metro fares receive a 20% discount, subject to the current policies of Tianfu Tong, Chengdu Metro, and Chengdu Bus services.

## 其他响应措施 — Other Response Measures

- For O3-dominated heavy pollution, promptly issue public health guidance, strengthen routine oversight of VOC and NOx sources, and tighten control of local sources such as catering fumes and renovation spraying.
- For uncontrollable factors such as dust, wildfire, or cross-border transport, promptly issue health guidance, direct the public to take protective action, and strengthen dust control and urban washing as conditions warrant.

## Terminology and Implementation Notes

- Health-protection measures advise the public how to reduce health risks during a warning.
- Voluntary emission-reduction measures encourage the public and organizations to reduce emissions on their own initiative.
- Mandatory emission-reduction measures legally control major mobile, industrial, and construction sources while maintaining essential city operations.
- Weekday plate-tail restrictions use 1 and 6 on Monday, 2 and 7 on Tuesday, 3 and 8 on Wednesday, 4 and 9 on Thursday, and 5 and 0 on Friday.
- Odd/even operation treats 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 as odd and 2, 4, 6, 8, and 0 as even.
- On the day an emergency warning is issued, vehicle restrictions and school suspension do not apply, and public-transport discounts are unavailable.
